The Umbilical Storyboard: Building an Interplanetary Supply Chain

We Can Reach Mars—But We Can’t Stay There.

Today, we have rockets powerful enough to reach Mars. But colonization requires more than launches. It requires fuel beyond Earth, economic self-sufficiency, a resilient supply chain, and interplanetary logistics. Without infrastructure, Mars is a one-way trip.

A Modular, Mobile, Nuclear-Powered Infrastructure

  • 🚀 Orbital Fueling Stations – Located around Earth, in-transit, and Mars orbit
  • ⚡ Nuclear Propulsion – Faster trips, lower costs, and high cargo throughput
  • 🛰️ Reusable, Relocatable Depots – Reconfigure to support asteroid mining or rescue
  • 🌐 Persistent Orbital Network – Active during and between Mars windows

Mining for Earth, On Mars

  • 🌱 Lower environmental destruction
  • ⚖️ No complex regulations or land rights
  • ♻️ Sustainable metal sourcing for Earth industries
  • 🔋 Strategic materials like uranium & rare earths
  • 💼 Attractive to ESG-aligned investors and governments

From Vision to Value: A 20-Year Payback Timeline

Total est. cost: ~$180B (phased). ROI begins around Year 20, enabled by earlier deployment of proven fission-based nuclear propulsion. Revenue streams include orbital services, Martian mining exports, and lunar relay operations.

We can build the economic lifeline that makes humanity multiplanetary—and profitable.
